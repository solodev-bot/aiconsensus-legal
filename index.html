Privacy Policy

AIConsensus Mobile · Last updated: 6 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what the AIConsensus Mobile app (the “App”) does with your information. This is the Google Play edition of the App. It is designed to work without an account and to keep your data on your own device.

1. No account, no sign-in

This edition does not require you to create an account or sign in. There is no login step and we do not operate a user profile or account for you inside the App.

2. Information stored on your device

The App stores the following information locally on your device, and it stays there unless you delete it:

  • Your chat history and the prompts and results you create in the App.
  • The API keys you enter for the AI providers you choose to connect. These are kept in the App’s private on-device storage; the active key is additionally kept in the platform’s encrypted storage.
  • Your App settings and preferences.

We do not upload this local data to us, because this edition has no account backend.

3. Requests to the AI providers you choose (BYOK)

The App is a “bring your own key” tool. When you send a prompt, it is transmitted directly from your device to the third-party AI provider(s) you configured, using your own API key. Those providers process the prompt under their own terms and privacy policies. The App is not an AI reseller and does not store your prompts on our servers.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of this edition is supported by advertising served through the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK. To serve ads, measure their performance, and prevent fraud, Google AdMob and its partners may collect and process data such as your device’s advertising identifier, device type, and general (non-precise) location. The App requests non-personalized ads and uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to obtain any consent required in your region before ads are requested. Google’s handling of this data is governed by Google’s own privacy policy. Users with an active PRO subscription do not see these ads.

5. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

An optional PRO subscription (product ID pro_yearly) can be purchased inside the App. Payments are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your card or payment details. When a purchase is confirmed, the App records a local entitlement on your device so it can unlock PRO features.

6. Third parties

  • Google AdMob — advertising for the free version (see section 4).
  • Google Play Billing — processing the optional PRO subscription (see section 5).
  • Your chosen AI providers — receive the prompts you send, under their own policies (see section 3).

We do not sell your personal information.

7. Retention

Local data (chat history, API keys, settings) remains on your device until you clear it in the App or uninstall the App. Advertising and purchase records are retained by Google according to Google’s own retention policies.

8. Your rights and deleting your data

Because your data is stored on your device, you are in control of it:

  • Use Settings → Clear Chat History to delete your local chat history.
  • Remove the API keys and providers you added in Settings.
  • Uninstalling the App removes the local data it stored on your device.

To request deletion of, or ask about, any data related to advertising or purchases, contact Google (as the relevant processor) or contact us using the address below and we will help where we can.

9. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be bundled with a new release of the App and will show a new “Last updated” date above.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact: aiconsensus.admin@gmail.com
